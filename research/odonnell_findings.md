# O'Donnell line: Joseph Francis O'Donnell (O'Donnell-1123) back to Ireland

Researched 18 November 2026 on FamilySearch (signed in) and Ancestry. Where I read a register image on screen, the entry says "image read". Nothing was downloaded.

Line: Mary O'Donnell (O'Donnell-1122, 1921–1978) ← **Joseph Francis O'Donnell** (1891–1928) ← **James W. O'Donnell** (1858/59–1926) & **Susan T. Mullen** ← **Jeremiah (Jerome) O'Donnell** (c.1835–1882) & **Eliza O'Hea** (c.1836–1876) ← Joseph O'Donnell & Mary; James O'Hea & Ann (Ireland).

## Short answer

- **James W. O'Donnell** (Joseph's father) was born in **August 1858–59 in the City of London** (parish of St Benet). His birth was registered as James William O'Donnell, City of London, July–Sept quarter 1859, vol. 1c p. 76. He was a brass finisher. On **21 Sep 1882** in Boston he married **Susan T. Mullen**, 21, born **Cambridge, Massachusetts**, daughter of **James and Catharine Mullen** (image read). He died **14 Jun 1926**, Boston, and was buried at St Joseph Cemetery, West Roxbury (Find a Grave index).
- **His parents were Jeremiah ("Jerome") O'Donnell, a tailor born in Ireland about 1835, and Eliza (O'Hea), born in Ireland about 1836–40.** They were in the **City of London in 1861** (census image read). Jeremiah is "Jeremiah" in the 1861, 1870 and 1880 censuses and on his own death record, and "Jerome" on James's 1882 marriage and on their son Joseph Jerome's 1868 birth.
- They emigrated **between 1861 and 1868** and lived in **Cambridge, Massachusetts** (1870, 1880).
- **Eliza died 7 May 1876 in Cambridge**, aged 36, born Ireland, daughter of **James O'Hea and Ann** (image read). Probable rather than proven, because the entry doesn't name her husband.
- **Jeremiah died 17 Nov 1882 in Boston**, a widowed tailor aged 47y 3m 7d (so born about Aug 1835), born Ireland, son of **Joseph O'Donnell and Mary** (image read). He was buried in Malden.
- **Children:** James W. (1859, London), Michael John (1861, London), Joseph Jerome (b. 22 May 1868, Boston/Cambridge), Julia (about 1871), Francis (about 1873) and Lizzie (about 1875).
- **Irish origin: not found.** No Irish county appears in any record seen. The parents probably married in London: the marriage index has a Jeremiah O'Donnell marrying at **St Martin in the Fields, Oct–Dec quarter 1856** (vol. 1a p. 558), and the bride on that page is indexed as "Elizabeth Delay / Le Lay". That could be a misreading of O'Hea, but it's unproven.
- **WikiTree:** no existing profiles for Jeremiah, Eliza, James W. (b. 1858/9) or Susan Mullen of Cambridge (API search, 18 Nov 2026).

## Open leads
1. **GRO certificates** (England, orderable online, about £12.50 each): James William O'Donnell's birth (City of London, Q3 1859, vol. 1c p. 76) gives the mother's maiden name and the address; the 1856 St Martin in the Fields marriage (vol. 1a p. 558) would confirm the bride and name both fathers. **These are the best paths to the Irish origin.**
2. **London Catholic registers** (Westminster diocese) for James's baptism, about 1859 (City of London chapels, e.g. St Mary Moorfields). These are on Findmypast, which needs a subscription.
3. **James W.'s 1926 Massachusetts death certificate** (FamilySearch "Massachusetts Deaths, 1841–1915" stops before 1926; the 1926 certificate is at the Massachusetts Archives / State Registry).
4. **Susan T. Mullen**: her Cambridge birth (about 1860–61), her parents James and Catharine Mullen in the 1865/1870 censuses, and her death.
5. **Jeremiah's naturalization** (Middlesex courts), if any. It might give an Irish county.
