# Ordering William E. Avery's Civil War pension file

## What to order
| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Veteran | **William E. Avery** |
| Service | Private, **Co. A, 17th New York Infantry** (enlisted 1861 at Yonkers; mustered out 2 Jun 1863) and **Co. G, 6th New York Heavy Artillery** (to 1865) |
| State | New York |
| War | Civil War, Union |
| Invalid (veteran's own) application | **No. 287,279**, filed 21 May 1879 |
| Widow's application | **No. 527,367**, filed 25 Sep 1891, New York. Widow: **Lizzie Avery** (née Hecker; formerly Mrs Weber) |
| Certificate numbers | None on the index card, so the claims may never have been approved. Give the application numbers instead. |
| Death | 26 Aug 1891, Manhattan (NYC death cert. 30213) |

Both applications should be in one combined file, but name both numbers on the order.

## How to order
1. **Check Fold3 first.** Search Fold3's "Civil War Pension Files" (also called "Civil War and Later Widows' Pension Files") for William E. Avery. Only a small share of files has been digitised, so it probably isn't there, but it's quick to check.
2. **Order from the National Archives.**
   - Go to the NARA eServices order site (eservices.archives.gov) and choose **"Federal Pension Applications"**, then **"Civil War and Later, Complete File"**. The order needs a free NARA account and payment, which you'll have to enter yourself.
   - Enter the details from the table above. Use the full application numbers in the file-number fields, and add the invalid application number in the comments.
   - Fees change, so check the current price on the site. A complete Civil War file is usually under $100 for the first 100 pages.
   - Ask for **digital delivery (PDF)** if it's offered. Expect a wait of several weeks to a few months.
3. **Cheaper or faster option:** pay an independent researcher at the National Archives building in Washington, DC to photograph the whole file. Pension files there are pulled on request, and this usually costs about the same or less and is faster.

## What to look for when it arrives
- **Lizzie's widow's declaration:**
  - whether William had been married before, to **Emily Louisa Carter**, and how that marriage ended (death or divorce)
  - the date and place of his marriage to Emily
- **The veteran's own 1879 application:**
  - his birth date and place
  - his residences since the war
  - his physical description
- **Affidavits from neighbours and relatives:** they may name his parents, **John Avery and Marietta Abbott**, or his sisters.
- **Children under 16 in 1891:** they may be listed, with birth dates.

Once it arrives, put the relevant pages in `records/`, which isn't committed, and I'll transcribe them into the drafts.
