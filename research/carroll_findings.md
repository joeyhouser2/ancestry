# Carroll / Hunt line: Mary Dolores (Carroll) McElligott's family

Researched 19 November 2026 on FamilySearch (signed in) and Ancestry. Where I read a register or certificate image on screen, the entry says "image read". Nothing was downloaded.

This is the Watertown Carroll line (Carroll-4441), not Ellen Carroll of the McGlynn line (see `mcglynn_findings.md`).

Line: Thomas J. McElligott (1919–1998) ← **Mary Dolores Carroll** (1880–) & James Thomas McElligott ← **James Carroll** (c.1836 Ireland – 1882?) & **Mary Hunt** (c.1837 Ireland – 1917) ← **James Hunt & Hannah Wren** (Ireland).

## Short answer
- **Mary Dolores Carroll was born 16 Feb 1880 in Watertown** and baptized there the next day (St Patrick's), daughter of James Carroll and Mary Hunt. She was the youngest of seven children.
  - WikiTree's 1884 birth year is wrong.
  - Her 1904 marriage gives 1880; the 1880 census lists her at 3 months.
- **Her mother, Mary (Hunt) Carroll, was born in Ireland about 1837.** She died in Watertown on 22 Oct 1917, a widow aged 80. Her death certificate names her parents as **James Hunt and Hannah Wren, both born in Ireland** (image read).
- **Her father, James Carroll, was born in Ireland about 1836** and was a teamster in Watertown in 1870–1880. He probably died there on 16 Jan 1882, aged 45; the register names his parents as **James and Catherine** (image read).
- **They were in Watertown by 1864**, when their first child was born. Their marriage has not been found in Massachusetts, so they may have married in Ireland.
- **Two Carroll brothers probably married two Hunt sisters:**
  - Michael Carroll (born Ireland about 1841, died Watertown 1901) was a son of James Carroll and Catherine **McCabe**.
  - Michael married **Johanna Hunt**.
  - In 1880, Michael's household included his mother-in-law **Hannah Hunt**, aged 65, born Ireland. She is probably Hannah (Wren) Hunt, Mary's mother.
  - If James and Michael were brothers, James's mother was Catherine McCabe. This is not proven.

## Follow-up, 19 Nov 2026 (later): the Hunt–Wren family in Worcester County
9. **Hannah (Wren) Hunt's death (image read).** "Massachusetts, U.S., Death Records, 1841-1915", Ancestry (https://www.ancestry.com/search/collections/2101/records/3888275 : accessed 19 November 2026); Watertown deaths 1896, entry 13 (image 1161 of 2206). Also on FamilySearch (https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:NWC8-DFS), vol. 464 p. 433.
   - Hannah (Wren) Hunt, **widow of James Hunt**, died **11 Feb 1896**, Watertown, aged 70; cause heart disease.
   - Born Ireland; parents **Michael [Wren] and Johannah Conners**, both born Ireland.
   - The FamilySearch index gives her burial place as **Worcester, Mass.**
   - Her age of 70 (born about 1826) is too young to be the mother of Mary (born about 1837). The 1865 census (50) and the 1880 census (65) both point to about 1815. The death register probably understates it.
   - Living in Watertown fits the 1880 census, where "Hannah Hunt, mother-in-law, 65" was in Michael and Johanna Carroll's household.
10. **Mary's sister Hannah Hunt was born in Rutland (image read).** Boston marriages 1904, p. 118, no. 2695 (FamilySearch, https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:N4ZD-23B : accessed 19 November 2026).
    - Married 15 Jun 1904: William H. McDonald, 51, teamster, born New Brunswick, widowed; and **Hannah Hunt**, 48, first marriage, of 79 Charles St., born **Rutland**.
    - Her parents are **James Hunt and Hannah Wren**.
    - Given the Worcester burial, this is almost certainly **Rutland, Worcester County, Massachusetts**, so the Hunts were in Massachusetts by about 1856.
11. **James Hunt's death, Paxton, 23 Jul 1874 (probable; image read).** "Massachusetts, U.S., Death Records, 1841-1915", Ancestry (https://www.ancestry.com/search/collections/2101/records/5417604 : accessed 19 November 2026); Paxton deaths 1874 (image 1083 of 1165).

12. **1865 Massachusetts census, Paxton (index only).** FamilySearch (https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:MQHB-J4L : accessed 19 November 2026), family 87.
    - James Hunt, 54, born Ireland; **Hannah Hunt, 50**, born Ireland.
    - Also Hannah, 1, and Alice, 3, both born Massachusetts. They are probably grandchildren, since Hannah senior was about 50.
13. **1855 Massachusetts census, Rutland (image read, inconclusive).** FamilySearch, ark 1:1:MQ4X-QVB.
    - A James Hunt, 40, born Ireland, with an unnamed wife, 35, and a child of 14.
    - Next to them is a separate Hunt household: Timothy, Susannah and Mary (3).
    - The ages don't clearly match ours.

**What this shows:** Mary Hunt's parents, James Hunt and Hannah Wren, emigrated from Ireland and farmed in the Rutland–Paxton area of Worcester County by the mid-1850s. James probably died at Paxton in 1874. Hannah then moved to Watertown to live with her daughters, and died there in 1896.

Paxton and Rutland are next to each other. Two things link the 1874 Paxton James to our family:
- the 1865 census couple James and Hannah in Paxton
- Hannah's Worcester burial

He is still "probable" until a record names his wife or children.

   - The church record of James and Mary's marriage, if it was in Ireland, would show the parish.
   - So might any Watertown St Patrick's register entry naming sponsors.
   - The 1917 certificate gives only "Ireland".
