# Avery and Carter line (Hattie Avery's parents): findings, 24 Nov 2026

Hattie (Avery) Bigg (1867–1911) is the mother of Alfred B. Bigg Jr (Bigg-111). Her parents were William Avery and Emily Louisa Carter. Earlier work is in the drafts and in `research/veterans_findings.md`. Nothing was downloaded.

## Short answer
- **William Avery's full name was William Edward Avery.** His son William Henry's 1871 NYC birth record names the parents as "William Edward" and "Emily Louisa Carter" (FamilySearch 1:1:27YF-RCC).
- **His parents were John Avery (born about 1809, NY) and Marietta Abbott (born about 1821, Danbury, Connecticut).**
  - In the **1850 census at Yonkers** he is William E. Avery, 6, with John 41, Marietta 29, and sisters Charlotte E., Mary A. and **Harriett M.** (FamilySearch 1:1:MCT9-LCH).
  - His **1880 Manhattan marriage** to Sisse (Lizzie) Weber née Hecker, a widow, names his parents as John and Marietta Abbott. I read the image: NYC cert. 7758, https://a860-historicalvitalrecords.nyc.gov/view/7709797.
  - The certificate gives him as a painter, born New York, aged 32 next birthday, and calls it his **second marriage**.
- **His 1891 death is now identified.** William E. Avery, painter, aged 48, born NY, married, of 606 W 37th St, died in Manhattan on **26 Aug 1891** and was buried at Weehawken, NJ. His father is given as John Avery (NYC Municipal Deaths, FamilySearch 1:1:2W6M-TZS, cert. 30213).
  - His widow Lizzie applied for his Civil War pension in Sep 1891.
  - He enlisted at Yonkers in 1861 (17th NY Infantry), which fits the Yonkers childhood.
- **Marietta Abbott's parents were Elijah P. Abbott and a mother née Benedict.** As Marietta Avery, born Danbury, Conn., she married John Ledbrook at Brooklyn on 13 May 1875 (cert. 871; FamilySearch 1:1:24Z2-X57). John Avery was presumably dead by then.

## How strong is the link to Hattie's father?
Strong, but indirect. No single record names Emily and John Avery together. These records agree:

| Point | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Hattie's father | William Avery, born NY, husband of Emily Louisa Carter (death 1911, baptisms 1873/74) |
| Name | His son's 1871 birth record: William Edward |
| Born c.1843–44 in NY | 1850 Yonkers census (6); 1891 death (48) |
| Yonkers | 1850 census; 1861 enlistment at Yonkers |
| Emily had remarried by 1880 | He calls Nov 1880 his **second** marriage |
| Same man in 1880 and 1891 | Painter in both; father John in both; widow Lizzie (Hecker) in the 1891 pension index |
| Family names | His daughters Mary Louise and Harriet; his sisters Mary A. and Harriett M. |

**What would prove it:** the widow's pension file (application 527,367, filed 25 Sep 1891, NARA). Pension files normally ask about earlier marriages and divorces.

## Emily Louisa Carter's parents
- Her father was Charles Carter (her 1924 death certificate); her mother is still unknown.
- FamilySearch's tree (L2HY-DCL) gives her mother as "Mary Pearson Carter" (PJWP-6HY), with **no source**.
- The 1884 NYC marriage attached to Emily in that tree is a different woman: Emily Kate Carter, born in England, daughter of Charles Carter and Caroline Robbins, who married Warren E. Smith.
- A FamilySearch search for Emily Carter born 1843–47 in New York, with father Charles, finds only the Marathon (Cortland Co.) family, which was already ruled out. A New York City Emily Carter with father Charles hasn't been found in 1850, 1855 or 1860 yet.
