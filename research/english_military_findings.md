# Mother's side, English lines: military service and the Markwell line

Researched 21 November 2026. This builds on `research/veterans_findings.md` (18 Nov), which searched
the pension-based indexes (TNA WO 97, WO 121, WO 96, ADM 139, ADM 29, ADM 73) and found nothing for the
Sheppey, Milton or Lincolnshire men. This round tried other record sets. Nothing was downloaded.

## Short answer

- **No direct ancestor on the English lines has a documented war record yet.** The men of fighting age
  in the French Revolutionary and Napoleonic Wars (1793–1815) were Richard Bigg (b. 1766), Thomas Shore
  (b. c.1760), Bartholomew Markwell (b. c.1774), and probably Thomas Jeffery and John Markwell. None of
  them appears in the records searched below. The most likely service, in the Kent or Lincolnshire
  militia, the Sea Fencibles or local volunteers, is in muster rolls that aren't name-indexed online.
- **A probable great-uncle served for twenty years in the cavalry:**
  - Samuel Markwell, born about 1778 at Horncastle, served in the 13th Light Dragoons from 1798 to 1818.
  - He is very probably a brother of Bartholomew, Joey's 6x-great-grandfather. He isn't a direct
    ancestor, so there is no draft.
  - The 13th Light Dragoons fought in the Peninsular War (1810–14) and at Waterloo.
  - His service papers (WO 97) would show whether he was at Waterloo; I haven't read the image.
- **The Markwell line goes back one more step, as a lead:** the probable parents John Markwell and
  Sarah West, and two candidates for Sarah's parents (below).

## Samuel Markwell (probable brother of Bartholomew)

- **Service record:** "UK, Royal Hospital Chelsea Pensioner Soldier Service Records, 1760-1920",
  Ancestry (https://www.ancestry.com/search/collections/61068/records/360219), citing TNA WO 97/90/77.
  - Samuel Markwell, born about 1778 at Horncastle, Lincolnshire.
  - He enlisted aged 20 in 1798 and was discharged aged 40 in 1818, from the 13th Regiment of Light
    Dragoons.
- **Pension:** TNA WO 121/183/125 (discharged 1818). The Chelsea admission books show him admitted to
  pension on 24 Sep 1818, and the regimental register of pensioners shows him at Horncastle on
  23 Sep 1818.
- **Burial:** "England, Select Deaths and Burials", Ancestry
  (https://www.ancestry.com/search/collections/9840/records/1938378): buried 8 Dec 1839 at Holbeach,
  Lincolnshire, aged 63.
- **Is he Bartholomew's brother?** That rests on three points; no single record names both men's parents.
  - Both were born at Horncastle in the 1770s.
  - The surname is rare there.
  - John and Sarah Markwell, Dissenters, had children registered at Horncastle (see below).
- **Ruled out:** a Samuel Markwell born about 1776 at Swaffham, Norfolk (1841 and 1851 censuses) is a
  different man.

## John Markwell & Sarah West (probable parents of Bartholomew)

From FreeREG transcripts of Horncastle St Mary's registers. The births below are of Dissenters' children,
entered in the parish register.

- **Marriage:** John Markwell, widower, and Sarah West, spinster, married by licence or banns on
  5 Sep 1769, register entry 180. The witnesses were **Frans West** and Thos Johnson.
- **Children of John & Sarah:**
  - John, born 1771, baptised as an adult on 30 Apr 1807.
  - William ("Wm"), born 10 Jun 1780, baptised as an adult on 30 Apr 1807. A William Markwell was
    buried at Horncastle on 10 Jul 1806, so that burial may be a different man.
  - Frances, born 27 Aug 1790, marked "a dissenter".
  - A Susanna, born 26 Mar 1786, was a daughter of John and **Jane**. That is either a transcription
    slip or a different John.
- **Missing:** no baptism or birth entry was found for Bartholomew (c.1773–74) or Samuel (c.1776–78).
  They probably appear only in the nonconformist register (TNA RG 4/1641).
- **Sarah West's baptism, two candidates at Horncastle:**
  1. Sarah, baptised 15 Dec 1749, daughter of **John & Mary West**. John West married Mary Dawson at
     Horncastle on 3 Nov 1740. This couple also had a daughter **Frances**, baptised 3 May 1747, which
     would make the 1769 witness "Frans West" the bride's sister. This is the better fit.
  2. Sarah, baptised 27 Jan 1741/2, daughter of **Wm & Mary West**. William West married Mary Houldon
     at Horncastle on 27 Dec 1738.
- **A will to check:** Nottinghamshire Archives DD/VC/39/3 (1780) is a copy of the will of "Ann West,
  wife of John W. of Horncastle (Lincs), leather cutter". If John West married again after Mary, this
  will may name a daughter Sarah Markwell.

## Record sets searched (21 Nov 2026), with results

- **Trafalgar Ancestors** (TNA database of everyone on the British side at Trafalgar, 1805), surnames
  Bigg, Biggs, Mantle, Shore, Ekins, Eakins, Markwell, Jeffery, Couchman, Shrubsall, Shrubsole, Oast:
  - Christopher Mantle, 35, born St George's, Somerset (HMS Tonnant). Not ours.
  - Joseph Shore, 45, born Kilkenny (HMS Prince). Not ours.
  - Some Biggs and Jefferys, none born in Kent or Lincolnshire.
- **TNA Discovery catalogue** (all archives), surname plus place (Minster, Eastchurch, Queenborough,
  Sheerness, Warden, Milton, Horncastle, Hameringham) and surname plus militia, seaman or marine:
  - **No military entries** for our men.
  - **Non-military Bigg finds at Minster**, possibly relatives of Richard Bigg (1766):
    - PCC will of John Bigg, gentleman, 1832 (PROB 11/1805/463).
    - Will of Sarah Bigg, widow, 1834 (PROB 11/1832/459).
    - Will of Edward Bigg, farmer, 1842 (PROB 11/1959/428).
    - Fire insurance policy of John Bigg, grazier, 1789 (London Archives MS 11936/362/560987).
    These may name Richard or his children. The will images are on TNA Discovery.
  - **A Sheerness Invalids man:** Thomas Biggs, 1st Foot Guards and Sheerness Invalids
    (WO 121/153/108, 1802), birthplace not known. Nothing links him to our Biggs.
- **Ancestry card catalogue:**
  - No Kent militia, Kent Sea Fencibles or Lincolnshire militia collection exists. The militia
    collections that do exist are Cornwall, Westminster and Worcestershire.
- **Kent parish registers on Ancestry** (collection 61746), Bigg burials 1785–1845: no burial for
  Richard Bigg (1766) found. Some online trees give him a death date of 21 Mar 1832, with no source.
