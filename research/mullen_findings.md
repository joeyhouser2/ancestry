# Mullen line: Susan T. (Mullen) O'Donnell's parents

Researched 18 November 2026 on FamilySearch (signed in). Where I read a register image on screen, the entry says "image read". Nothing was downloaded.

Line: Joseph F. O'Donnell (1891–1928) ← **Susan T. Mullen** (1857–1941?) ← **James Mullen** (c.1829 Charlestown – 1905) & **Catherine McElaney** (c.1828 East Cambridge – 1872) ← **Patrick Mullen & Ellen Loan** (Ireland) and **Thomas & Susan McElaney** (Ireland).

## Short answer
- **Susan T. Mullen was born 24 Aug 1857 in Cambridge, Massachusetts** (image read). Her 1882 marriage gave her age as 21, which would mean about 1861, but brides often shaved years off. The birth register's parents (James and Catherine), town and the father's trade all fit.
- **Her parents were born in Massachusetts, not Ireland**, a generation earlier than the McElligott and O'Donnell immigrants:
  - **James Mullen**: born **Charlestown about 1829**, coffin maker (1857), later an **undertaker**; died a widower in **Boston, 8 Dec 1905**, aged 76. His parents were **Patrick Mullen and Ellen Loan, both born in Ireland**.
  - **Catherine (McElaney / McLaney) Mullen**: born **East Cambridge about 1828**; died in **Boston, 13 Oct 1872**, aged 44. Her parents were **Thomas and Susan McElaney, both born in Ireland**. Susan was evidently named after this grandmother.
- **Their marriage (probable):** James Mullin and Catherine McLaney, 24 Aug 1851, Sandwich, Barnstable County. The link rests on the names only.
- **The Irish generation:** Patrick Mullen & Ellen Loan were in Charlestown by 1829, and Thomas & Susan McElaney were in East Cambridge by 1828. Irish emigrants this early (before the Famine) are less common and easier to pick out in records. A daughter, **Ellen (Mullen) Moran**, has a death in the index naming Patrick Mullen and Ellen Lone.

## Follow-up, 18 Nov 2026 (later): Patrick Mullen & Ellen Loan's family
8. **1850 census, Cambridge (image read).** "1850 United States Federal Census", Ancestry (https://www.ancestry.com/search/collections/8054/records/10383898 : accessed 18 November 2026); Cambridge, Middlesex, roll 325, p. 73a, dwelling 1048, family 1180 (image 145 of 363).
   - **Ellen Mullen**, 52, born Ireland, real estate $500, cannot read.
   - **Rosanna**, 25, born **Ireland**.
   - **Mary**, 21, born Massachusetts.
   - **James**, 19, born Massachusetts, engineer in a foundry.
   - **William**, 18, born Massachusetts, nail maker.
   - **Ellen** Mullen 17 and **Ellen Lane** 10 are on the next page (index only). "Lane" is possibly the same name as Ellen's maiden name Loan/Lone, so the child may be a niece.
   - **What it shows:** Patrick is absent, so he probably died before 1850. Rosanna's Irish birth (about 1825) and the Massachusetts births from about 1829 put the emigration between **about 1825 and 1829**. This James is probably ours (Cambridge, born Massachusetts, about the right age), but this rests on the household's names and ages, not on a record naming his parents.
9. **Ellen Mullen's death (probable).** "Massachusetts, U.S., Death Records, 1841-1915", Ancestry (https://www.ancestry.com/search/collections/2101/records/7353579 : accessed 18 November 2026): Ellen Mullen, died **15 Dec 1860, Cambridge**, aged 62 (born about 1798), born Ireland; parents **John and Ellen**, presumably John and Ellen Loan. The register image was opened but not yet read.
10. **Ellen (Mullen) Moran**, sister of James: died **15 Nov 1898, Cambridge**, aged 64 (born about 1834); wife of Joseph; parents Patrick Mullen and Ellen Lone ("Massachusetts, Deaths and Burials, 1795-1910", FamilySearch, https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:FH2D-NX4 : accessed 18 November 2026).
11. **Patrick Mullen's death:** not found in Massachusetts deaths for 1841–1850. He may have died before statewide registration began in 1841, or under a variant spelling.
