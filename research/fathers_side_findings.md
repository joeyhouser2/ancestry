## Catherine Drost (Drost-122)

- **1899 arrival** (image read): S.S. Pisa, Hamburg to New York, arrived 21 Jan 1899, line 5.
  - "Catha Drost", 14, single, Austrian, last residence Dukla, destination Hartford, Conn.
  - She travelled "with no. 3": Wojciech Sładyk of Dukla, also bound for Hartford, who was joining a cousin.
  - She was going to a brother. His name is hard to read, possibly "Jozef Sch…".
- **Birth year:** age 14 puts her birth about 1884–85. WikiTree, the 1940 census and her grave say 1880.
- **Geneteka (Dukla – Fara births, village Cergowa):** Marianna Drost 1861 (no. 19) and Antoni Drost 1864 (no. 17), parents Szymon Drost & Barbara Mozdzan.
  - Dukla births after 1864 are not indexed, so her own baptism isn't reachable there.
- **Next:** her Connecticut marriage record (c.1900–02) and her 1942 Connecticut death certificate; the Dukla register for about 1884.

## Rupert Bąk & Joanna Chłap (Bak-186, Chlap-2)

- **Geneteka, Jasionka marriages 1866, no. 2** (scan linked from the index):
  - Rupert Bąk, son of Szymon & Jadwiga Mozdzan.
  - Joanna Chłap, daughter of Antoni & Regina Jakieła.
- **Jasionka births:** Franciszek 1869 (13), Katarzyna 1871 (21), Andrzej 1873 (24), Barbara 1875 (35), Maciej 1879 (5). The father is indexed as "Paufert?" in 1871 and 1873.
- **A discrepancy to check:** Chłap births at Jasionka in 1856 (Marianna, no. 10) and 1859 (Małgorzata, no. 31) give the parents as **Wojciech** Chłap & Regina Jakieła. Either the given name is indexed differently or Regina married twice.
- **No parents' marriage indexed:** none for Szymon Bąk or Antoni Chłap in podkarpackie, 1820–1850.
- **Open question on Joseph Mercik:** Mercik-2 (b. 6 Jul 1869 Jasionka) is linked to Rupert as the son Franciszek. But the family email says Franciszek Bąk married Tekla Gąsior and had children in Poland in 1895–1900. Joseph Mercik was in Connecticut by 1903, and the WikiTree note puts his arrival about 1888. Worth checking before relying on the link.

## Thomas Bunce & Bridget Conlan (Bunce-393, Conlan-245)

- **Thomas:** Brooklyn death certificate 5808 (1895), a coroner's certificate (image read).
  - Died 30 Mar 1895 at St Peter's Hospital, of shock and injuries from a fall; inquest 1 Apr; registered 2 Apr.
  - Age 57, born Ireland, foreman of stores, 146 Coffey St (Ward 12). Parents born Ireland, names not asked.
- **Bridget:** Brooklyn death certificate 5720 (1892), image read.
  - Died 7 Apr 1892 at 62 Underhill Ave (Ward 9), of phthisis.
  - Age 41, married, housewife, born Ireland, 26 years in the US. Parents born Ireland, names not asked.
- **Their marriage:** not in the NYC marriage index 1866+ under Thomas Bunce.
- **Other leads:** FamilySearch has a Thomas Bunce, born Ireland, naturalized in New York in 1867, and NYC births for Thomas Pat. Bunce (1 Dec 1872, parents Thomas & Bridget Conlon) and James (1871).

## James Moore & Ann (Wallace) (Moore-35142, Unknown-381847)

- **Rose M. Bunce:** Brooklyn death certificate 11804 (1909), image read.
  - Died 22 Jun 1909, age 37, married, born US.
  - Father James Moore (born US). **Mother's maiden name Ann Wallace**, born Ireland.
- **James Moore:** Brooklyn death certificate 21090 (1904), image read.
  - Died 30 Oct 1904 at 245 Sixth Ave, age 72, **widowed**, retired.
  - Born **Ireland**, 60 years in the US. Father Patrick Moore, mother Rose Cox, both born Ireland.
- **Wrong match ruled out:** Ann Moore, d. 23 Mar 1914 Brooklyn (cert 6388), b. 15 Aug 1842 Ireland, is **not** her. Her father was James Torry, her mother Ellen Mullins.
