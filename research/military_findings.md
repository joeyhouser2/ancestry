## Thomas McElligott: Civil War search (17 Nov 2026)

**Conclusion: no record fits our Thomas.** Our Thomas was a son of Patrick and Catharine, could not read or write in 1870, and lived in Charlestown MA. Probably he arrived around or after the war's end.

Searched, with results:
- **"U.S., Civil War Soldier Records and Profiles, 1861-1865"** (Ancestry coll. 1555). One exact match: Thomas McElligott, age 21 (b. abt 1840), enlisted 11 Oct 1861 at **Bouckville, NY**. He served in Co. D, 10th NY Cavalry, was promoted sergeant in 1864 and quartermaster sergeant later in 1864, transferred to the 1st NY Provisional Cavalry, and mustered out 19 Jul 1865 at Cloud's Mills, VA ([record 1578969](https://www.ancestry.com/search/collections/1555/records/1578969)). **Probably a different man:** he served upstate, and a quartermaster sergeant had to be literate, which our Thomas was not.
- **"U.S., Civil War Soldiers, 1861-1865"** (NPS index, coll. 1138), wildcards `Mc*lig*t*`, `*ligot*`, `*lagot*`, `*licot*`. Found only the same 10th NY Cavalry / 1st NY Provisional Cavalry man, plus a **Thomas Elligott, 2nd Regiment Veteran Reserve Corps** (not followed up; low probability).
- **"U.S., Civil War Pension Index"** (coll. 4654), `Mc*lig*t*`:
  - Thomas McElligot, alias John Flynn, Co. F 12th US Infantry and Navy. Mother's pension application 548056, filed 14 Apr 1892, NY. **Ruled out:** the dependent mother was **Bridget** McElligot, and ours was Catharine's son. (Card image viewed.)
  - Thomas McElligott, filed 13 Oct 1888, Minnesota. Matches the Medford, Minnesota veteran below. Ruled out.
- **"1890 Veterans Schedules"** (coll. 8667), `Mc*lig*t*`. The Massachusetts schedule survives, and it has **no Thomas McElligott in Boston**. The only Thomas is a sergeant in Medford, Steele Co., Minnesota. This is good negative evidence, if our Thomas was alive in 1890 (last seen 1881).

Not yet checked:
- *Massachusetts Soldiers, Sailors, and Marines in the Civil War* (the state's official roster).
- US Navy enlistments. Many Boston Irish served in the Navy, and the navy isn't well covered by the indexes above.

### Side find, not military: may help find the Irish home parish
The general Ancestry search showed **"New York, U.S., Emigrant Savings Bank Records, 1850-1883"** entries for a Thomas McElligott born in Kerry:
- b. 1844 Kerry, 9 Jul 1864 (indexed as "Thomas W Mcelligott");
- b. 1844 Kerry, 19 Oct 1865;
- b. 1841 Kerry, 19 Nov 1867.

The bank's test books often record the depositor's parents, home parish and arrival ship. These are not proven to be our Thomas, who was in Charlestown by 1868. They are worth opening alongside lead #14 in [OPEN_LEADS.md](OPEN_LEADS.md).
