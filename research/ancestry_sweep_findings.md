# Ancestry sweep, mother's side (23 Nov 2026)

The goal was to get everything useful on the mother's side out of Ancestry while the subscription is
active. Every finding below is transcribed with its record link, so a future subscription isn't needed to
know what each record says. The drafts already carry these citations. Nothing was downloaded.

## New findings

### Katharina (Motzer) Höhn, Jacob Höhn's first wife: death found
- **Record:** Hesse, Germany, Deaths, 1851-1958 (Ancestry
  https://www.ancestry.com/search/collections/61119/records/1978219). Standesamt Königstein 1886, no. 23.
  Image read.
- **Contents:**
  - Katharina Höhn, born Motzer, died **14 Aug 1886 at Königstein im Taunus**, aged 37, Protestant.
  - She lived at Königstein and was born at Eppstein.
  - The entry says she was "verheirathet mit dem zur Zeit abwesenden Gastwirth Jacob Höhn": married to the
    innkeeper Jacob Höhn, who was then away.
  - Her parents were the late Georg Wilhelm Motzer and Maria Johannette Auguste.
- **What it settles:**
  - Katharina did not emigrate. Jacob left her in Germany in 1883 and lived in New York with Margaretha
    Schmidt.
  - Katharina's death made him a widower, so he could marry Margaretha legally in 1896.
  - Anna is still Margaretha's daughter: the 1900 census says Margaretha had borne two living children,
    Adam and Anna.
- **Draft:** `drafts/Hoene-55.json` is updated.

### Thomas & Susan McElaney (Catherine Mullen's parents): 1850 household found
- **Record:** 1850 census, Sandwich, Barnstable County, Massachusetts (Ancestry
  https://www.ancestry.com/search/collections/8054/records/9894447), dwelling 287, family 304.
- **Household:**
  - Thomas McElleney, 46, butcher, born Ireland, real estate worth $600.
  - Susan, 46, born Ireland.
  - Catherine, 21; Susan, 19; Ann, 17; Thomas P., 7. All four children were born in Massachusetts.
  - Daniel Camell, 19, born England.
- **Why it's ours:** it fits Catherine's 1851 marriage to James Mullin at Sandwich and her 1872 death
  record (parents Thomas and Susan, both born in Ireland). The family was in Massachusetts by about 1829.
- **Not found:** Thomas's and Susan's deaths, searched in Massachusetts deaths 1841–1915 under
  McElaney, McElleney and McEl*.
- **Draft:** `drafts/NEW-McElaney-Catherine.json` is updated.

### Correction: Mary Bigg (Bigg-424) is not Richard Bigg & Mary Mantle's daughter
- **Record:** the will of **Sarah Bigg of Minster, widow**, dated 27 Jan 1820 and proved 19 Jun 1834
  (PCC, PROB 11/1832; Ancestry https://www.ancestry.com/search/collections/5111/records/120207). Image
  read.
- **Contents:**
  - It names her daughter **Mary, wife of William Rouse**.
  - Her sons William Bigg and Edward Bigg are trustees.
  - Her daughter Sarah Bigg, spinster, is sole executrix.
- **Why this matters:** Mary Bigg married William Rouse at Eastchurch in March 1812, so Mrs Rouse is
  Sarah's daughter. The Mary baptised in 1796 to Richard and Mary is a different girl. She would have been
  only 15 in 1812.
- **What to do on WikiTree:** don't attach Bigg-424 to Richard & Mary (Mantle). Treat Bigg-398 (Sarah,
  Mrs Claringbold) as unproven too: the widow Sarah also had a daughter Sarah.
- **Draft:** `drafts/NEW-Bigg-RichardSr-MaryMantle.json` is updated.
- **Still standing:** John Bigg's 1832 will was already cited and still proves Richard Sr's parents
  (Edward Bigg & Mary Couchman).

### Hattie Avery's baptism sponsor
- St Andrew's, Harlem, 27 Nov 1874 (Episcopal Diocese of New York records; image read).
- Hattie (born 18 Aug 1867) and William Henry (born 3 Apr 1871) were sponsored by **Cornelia Pennoyer**
  and their mother.
- Cornelia R. Pennoyer's 1884 New York will names her father as Edward G. Thompson, so she was not a
  Carter. She was probably a family friend, and no help with Emily Carter's parents.

### McElligott
- **The three Emigrant Savings Bank entries are ruled out** (images read): 9 Jul 1864, 19 Oct 1865 and
  19 Nov 1867.
  - All three are one New York man: Thomas McElligott, born 1844 in Co. Kerry, who arrived on 24 Nov 1862
    on the *City of Washington*.
  - He was a labourer and porter, living at 145 Leonard St and later 53 Baxter St, and was married to
    **Mary Stack**.
  - Our Thomas married Mary Sullivan in Charlestown in 1868, so this isn't him.
- **A collateral relative found:** Patrick McElligott, son of Thomas & Mary (Sullivan), born 23 Mar 1870
  and baptised 25 Mar 1870 at Charlestown (Boston Archdiocese index, Ancestry 61585).
- **The Boston Archdiocese index on Ancestry** gives names, dates and parish only; the images are on
  American Ancestors.
  - It confirms the 11 Oct 1881 Boston marriage of Thomas McElligott and Anna Nash.
  - It shows no witnesses.
  - It doesn't cover the pre-1860 Charlestown and Cambridge registers we need for the Mullens.
